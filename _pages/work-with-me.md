I work with tissue imaging and clinical research groups in two ways, described below. If you are not sure which one fits, describe the project and I will suggest a track. In general I am interested in working with domain experts who want to explore complex data structures, whether or not they run the analyses themselves.

<div class="work-tracks">

<div class="work-track">
  <h2>Consulting</h2>
  <p>This track is for teams that need a specific analysis done well and soon. I scope the work and deliver publication-ready results and code. You keep full ownership, and no authorship is expected.</p>
  <p><strong>Good fit when:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>You have imaging or spatial data and a defined analysis question.</li>
    <li>You need spatial statistics beyond the standard pipelines.</li>
    <li>You want reproducible code and figures that can go directly into a paper or report.</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong>How it works:</strong> a short scoping call, a fixed price for a defined scope, and an agreed timeline.</p>
  <p><a href="mailto:bhadury@upenn.edu?subject=Consulting%20inquiry" class="btn btn--primary">Start a consulting project</a></p>
</div>

<div class="work-track">
  <h2>Research collaboration</h2>
  <p>This track is for projects that need new methodology and lead to a paper. I contribute methods development and analysis as a co-author, and we share data and credit.</p>
  <p><strong>Good fit when:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Your question needs a method that does not exist yet, or a real extension of one of mine.</li>
    <li>You are aiming for a strong journal and want a methods co-author on the team.</li>
    <li>You can share the data and are set up for a joint project.</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong>How it works:</strong> we talk through the question, agree on scope and authorship up front, and run it as a joint project.</p>
  <p><a href="mailto:bhadury@upenn.edu?subject=Collaboration%20proposal" class="btn btn--primary">Propose a collaboration</a></p>
</div>

</div>

## What I do not take on

I take a small number of projects at a time. Routine analysis that a general bioinformatics core can handle is usually better placed there. I focus on spatial and Bayesian modeling that a core facility does not usually offer.
